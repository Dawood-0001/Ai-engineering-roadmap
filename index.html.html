David Dennis · From BSCS to Full-Stack AI Engineer
You do not need to know everything on Day 1, and you will not become an AI engineer overnight. Compound your skills over years: learn, build, break things, debug, ship, get internships, work with teams, study AI deeply, build production systems, keep improving. A degree gives the foundation; projects show ability; internships give experience; DSA prepares interviews; AI gives depth; cloud enables deployment; system design builds architectural thinking; communication creates impact.
